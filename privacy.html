Privacy Policy

Personal OAuth Application · Last updated: September 15, 2026

1. About this application

This OAuth client ("the Application") is a personal, non-commercial tool developed and operated by an individual user for the sole purpose of authenticating with Google APIs (such as Google Drive and Gmail) on behalf of authorized accounts.

2. Data accessed

When you authorize the Application, it requests access to the Google services you approve. Depending on the scopes granted, the Application may read or write:

  • Files and folders in your Google Drive
  • Emails in your Gmail account
  • Basic profile information associated with your Google account

3. How data is used

Accessed data is used exclusively to perform automated operations on behalf of the authorized user. The Application does not sell, share, or disclose your data to any third party for advertising, marketing, or analytics purposes.

4. Data storage and security

OAuth tokens are stored as encrypted environment variables inside private serverless workers (Cloudflare Workers) and are never persisted in plain text, logged, or exposed through public endpoints. Accessed content is processed transiently to perform the requested operations.

5. Third-party services

The Application only communicates with Google APIs and the infrastructure required to execute authorized operations (a Cloudflare Worker and a private database owned by the operator). No additional third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services are used.

6. Revoking access

You may revoke the Application's access to your Google account at any time by visiting myaccount.google.com/permissions and removing the Application from the list of authorized apps. Upon revocation, all stored tokens become invalid immediately.

7. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the Application's functionality or legal requirements. The current version will always be published on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or to request the deletion of any data processed by the Application, contact the Application operator through the Google account associated with the OAuth client.
